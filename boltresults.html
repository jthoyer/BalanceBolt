Series standings

Your best four races of the five. Finish four to be in the running for the grand prize.

How the series is scored

  1. Best four of five. We count your four best races. Turn up to all five and your worst one is dropped — one shocker won't sink your series.
  2. Finish four to qualify. Anyone on fewer than four finished races isn't in the running for the grand prize, however good those races were.
  3. Smallest total wins. Add up the differences from your best four. Closest to your own calls takes the grand prize.

Differences are absolute — a minute under your call costs exactly as much as a minute over it. Every race still hands out its own prizes on the day, qualified or not.
